# Sprint 6 Streaming closeout

Audit date: 2026-11-26. Implementation baseline: `5c2c863` on `main`, after [PR #253](https://github.com/Duyphanb/Self-hosted-VoD-Platform/pull/253). The closeout is tracked in [#256](https://github.com/Duyphanb/Self-hosted-VoD-Platform/issues/256).

Sprint 6 Streaming has thirteen of its fourteen DoD items verified, within the limits stated at the end. Twelve rest on evidence from the merged implementation; the manual close-and-resume item rests on hand-run rows on branch stacks, with the same path automated on `main` by the Chrome smoke. The fourteenth, a Postman collection for the playback and progress endpoints, is not met; [#254](https://github.com/Duyphanb/Self-hosted-VoD-Platform/issues/254) tracks it, and other checks cover parts of those endpoints: the CI stack smoke calls playback info, the playlist and a segment through Nginx, the Chrome smoke saves progress and resumes after a reload, and progress reads and Continue Watching are covered by the PostgreSQL integration tests, with a manual check of the Continue Watching API and page on the #243 branch stack (see the DoD table). Each item maps to the backlog issues below. This is verification evidence, not a replacement for the frozen contracts ([ADR-011](../architecture/adr/ADR-011.md) D1-D18 with its addenda, [ADR-012](../architecture/adr/ADR-012.md) and the documents they amend).

Sprint 6 proves that a video uploaded through Nginx and encoded by the real worker plays in desktop Google Chrome through the authenticated backend HLS proxy, that playback survives an access-token expiry through one shared refresh, and that progress is saved and resumed after a reload. It does not establish playback on the A1 VM or behind HTTPS, playback while an encode runs, or the capacity of the target host.

## Acceptance criteria

Where the evidence runs:
- **B-mvn:** `mvn verify` in the required **Backend Maven build and test** job. Full Spring context tests run on PostgreSQL 16 with the Flyway migrations, and the auto-configured MockMvc suites validate the responses of contract operations against `API-CONTRACT.yaml`, except requests that a test explicitly marks off-contract.
- **FE:** the required **Frontend build** job: lint, Vitest, the production build and the Playwright `test:e2e` suite (`session.spec.ts` and `player.spec.ts` against fixture backends, in Chromium and Firefox).
- **STACK:** the required **Skeleton build checks** job. `deploy/tests/worker-stack-smoke.py` uploads a generated clip through Nginx, waits for READY and checks playback info, the playlist and a segment through Nginx. The auth runtime smokes and the three Newman collections run in the same step.
- **CHROME:** the **Chrome playback smoke** workflow (`workflow_dispatch`): [run 45665737701](https://github.com/Duyphanb/Self-hosted-VoD-Platform/actions/runs/45665737701) on `main` at `5c2c863`, Google Chrome 158.0.8127.88 on a GitHub-hosted Linux runner. A generated 120-second H.264/AAC clip is uploaded through Nginx, encoded by the real worker and played on the real watch page with a one-minute access token. The [#125 closeout](https://github.com/Duyphanb/Self-hosted-VoD-Platform/issues/125#issuecomment-7182192384) maps its checks.
- **MANUAL:** browser rows recorded on [PR #242](https://github.com/Duyphanb/Self-hosted-VoD-Platform/pull/242) against a disposable Compose stack built from that branch, with a real 150-second upload encoded by the worker; the stack check on [PR #243](https://github.com/Duyphanb/Self-hosted-VoD-Platform/pull/243).
